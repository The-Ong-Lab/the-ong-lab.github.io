The people behind the work

Principal investigator

Charlene J. Ong, MD, MPHS

  • Associate Professor of Neurology and Neurosurgery, Boston University Chobanian & Avedisian School of Medicine
  • Division Chief, Neurocritical Care, Boston Medical Center
  • Director, VITAL ARC

Charlene Ong is a neurointensivist and physician-scientist who develops data-driven tools to detect neurologic decline and predict recovery in critically ill patients. Her research combines quantitative pupillometry, eye tracking, electrooculography, non-invasive intracranial pressure monitoring and machine learning to find early, objective signals of brain injury and recovery after stroke, traumatic brain injury and cardiac arrest.

Her EDEMA score is widely used to predict life-threatening swelling after stroke, and her group published the first machine learning algorithm to detect eye movements using electrooculography. Her work has been supported by NINDS, the American Heart Association, the Doris Duke Charitable Foundation and the BU Hariri Institute, and she directs VITAL ARC, a BU consortium for sensor-enabled AI in critical illness.

She trained at the University of Pennsylvania, Columbia, Washington University in St. Louis and the MGH and Brigham neurocritical care fellowship. She serves on the editorial board of Neurocritical Care and received the 2025 Excellence in Research Mentorship Award from the BU MD Program.
